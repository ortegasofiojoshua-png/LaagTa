About This Destination

Kumaykay River Farm is a serene agri-tourism and nature retreat tucked along the banks of the Kumaykay River in Barangay Dalirig, Manolo Fortich, Bukidnon. Surrounded by vibrant gardens, towering trees, and cool mountain waters, it offers visitors a quiet escape from the city.

Guests can stroll through landscaped flower and herb gardens, enjoy fresh farm-to-table meals, and unwind in cozy riverside cottages beside the gentle flow of the river.
