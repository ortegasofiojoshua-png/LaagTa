About This Destination

Located in the heart of Barangay Tankulan, the Manolo Fortich Municipal Plaza serves as the lively civic and social center of the municipality, featuring open lawns, shaded walkways, and community landmarks.

Framed by well-kept paths, benches, and local monuments, the plaza comes alive in the late afternoon and evening with families, joggers, and local street food vendors—making it an ideal spot to experience everyday local life in Manolo Fortich.
