Reflect and Honor at Mangima Heroes Shrine

Mangima Heroes Shrine stands as a solemn tribute to bravery and service, surrounded by the lush greenery of Mangima Canyon in Manolo Fortich, Bukidnon. Built to commemorate the Filipino soldiers and local freedom fighters who defended the region during World War II, its quiet, respectful ambiance makes it a meaningful stop for visitors exploring the area's historical sites.

Framed by crisp highland air, dense foliage, and the winding stretch of Sayre Highway, the memorial showcases life-sized sculptures portraying soldiers and locals in historic scenes. Today, it serves both as a peaceful place for remembrance and a scenic roadside stop where travelers can pause, take photos, and appreciate the heritage of northern Bukidnon.
