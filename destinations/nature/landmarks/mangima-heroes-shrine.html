About This Destination

Mangima Heroes Shrine is a historical landmark and memorial situated along the scenic Mangima Canyon in Manolo Fortich, Bukidnon. Built to honor the courage and sacrifice of Filipino soldiers and local freedom fighters who defended the region during World War II, the shrine stands as a lasting tribute to Bukidnon's wartime heritage.

Surrounded by lush mountain greenery, crisp highland air, and the winding roads of Sayre Highway, the site features life-sized commemorative sculptures depicting soldiers and locals in historical scenes. Today, it serves as both a solemn place of remembrance and a popular stopover where travelers can reflect on local history, take photos, and enjoy the natural beauty of northern Bukidnon.
